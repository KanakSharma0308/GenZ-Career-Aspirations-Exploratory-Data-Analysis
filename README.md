# GenZ-Career-Aspirations-Exploratory-Data-Analysis
📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes survey data collected from Gen Z respondents to uncover patterns and trends in:
🌍 Geographic distribution of respondents
🎯 Factors that influence career aspirations
🎓 Willingness to pursue higher education abroad (self-funded)
🏢 Likelihood of staying with a single employer for 3+ years
💼 Most preferred working environments
The goal is to understand what drives the career decisions of today's young workforce and present findings through clean, interactive visualizations.

Key Insights

A significant portion of Gen Z respondents are based in India, reflecting the dataset's regional focus.
Passion and personal interest rank among the top factors driving career choices for Gen Z.
A notable segment is willing to self-fund higher education abroad, showing strong ambition.
Gen Z leans toward shorter employer tenures, reflecting the job-hopping culture of the modern era.
Hybrid and remote work environments are strongly preferred over traditional in-office setups.
